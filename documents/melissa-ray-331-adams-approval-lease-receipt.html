2. Property Description

The Premises is a pet-friendly, moderately furnished residence of approximately 816 sq ft, built in 1940, with 3 bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms. The home includes a kitchen with eating space, living room, utility room, one covered parking space, ceramic tile and laminate flooring, a block foundation, and a metal roof.

The fixed lease term begins on August 1, 2026 and ends on January 31, 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease and applicable Washington law.

Monthly rent is $1,100.00, due on or before the first day of each month unless another due date is confirmed in writing by management.

The application fee of $75.00 has been paid and is acknowledged in the enclosed receipt. The application fee is separate from rent and security deposit unless management states otherwise in writing.

5. Use, Occupancy, and Conduct

The Premises shall be used only as a private residential dwelling by the named Tenant and no more than 3 approved occupants unless written permission is provided by Landlord or management. Tenant shall not assign this Lease, sublet the Premises, or allow unauthorized long-term occupants without prior written consent.

Tenant shall keep the Premises clean, sanitary, and free from waste, nuisance, illegal activity, or conduct that disturbs neighbors or damages the property.

Tenant is responsible for arranging and paying all utilities and routine services for the Premises unless Landlord or management confirms a different arrangement in writing. Tenant shall keep utilities active as needed to protect the Premises from damage, including reasonable heating during cold weather.

The Premises is moderately furnished and includes a dishwasher, dryer, refrigerator, range, washer, and electric water heater. One covered parking space is included. Tenant vehicle on record: 2010 Ford Fusion. Tenant may not store abandoned, inoperable, leaking, or unregistered vehicles on the Premises without written permission.

Tenant shall promptly notify management of needed repairs, leaks, safety concerns, pest concerns, or damage. Non-emergency requests may be sent to support@privatepropmanagement.com. Landlord or management may enter the Premises as permitted by law for inspections, repairs, maintenance, emergencies, or other lawful purposes.

If Tenant fails to pay rent, complete required deposit payments, violates this Lease, damages the Premises, or fails to comply with applicable law, Landlord may pursue all lawful remedies, including notices, lease termination, eviction, recovery of unpaid amounts, repair costs, fees, and other lawful charges.
