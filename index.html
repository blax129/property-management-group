How the Rental Process Works

  1. 01

    Inquire About a Listing

    Contact us about the rental you're interested in and confirm availability before applying.

  2. 02

    Submit Application

    Complete the rental application form with accurate information.

  3. 03

    Refundable Application Fee Payment

    A refundable application fee is a payment that will be returned to you if your application is not approved or if you decide not to move forward after the tour. Refund requests are processed within an hour of receiving your request, provided the application has not been approved.

  4. 04

    Next Steps

    Qualified applicants will be contacted with updates, tour details, or further instructions.

Why the application fee?

A clear, refundable step that keeps the review process organized for every applicant.

What it covers

The application fee supports screening, verification, and direct review of your rental application by our property management team.

When it is refundable

The fee is returned if your application is not approved or if you choose not to move forward after your property tour.

If you are approved

When approved and you decide to lease the home, the application fee is applied toward your first month’s rent.

Refund policy

Refund requests are processed within an hour of receiving your request, provided the application has not been approved.

Rental Process FAQ

How do I apply for a rental?

You can apply by completing the rental application form on this website.

Is there an application fee?

Yes, there is a refundable application fee of $75, which is paid through Chime after your application is submitted. The fee supports application screening and review. It is refundable if your application is not approved or if you choose not to move forward after your tour.

How quickly are application fee refunds processed?

Refund requests are processed within an hour of receiving your request, provided the application has not been approved.

Are listings always available?

Not always. Rental availability may change at any time.

How will I be contacted after applying?

Applicants are typically contacted using the email address or phone number provided in the application form.
